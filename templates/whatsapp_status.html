Checklist when the name does not show
  1. Name status above must be APPROVED and the account mode must be LIVE.
  2. Business verification must be Verified in Meta Business Settings → Security Center. An unverified business shows only the number.
  3. If you changed the name, re-register the phone number after approval (within 14 days), otherwise the old name stays.
  4. Test on a phone that has never chatted with the number and has not saved it. A saved contact name always wins over your display name.
  5. The name must match your business name and website (for example “SwiftInvite” with a SwiftInvite website or page listed in the verified business).
  6. Still not showing? Open a Meta Business Support request (message template in the chat).
Stop regenerating the token (do this once)
  1. Meta Business Settings → Users → System users → Add (role: Admin).
  2. Add assets: your app (Full control) and your WhatsApp account (Full control).
  3. Generate token → pick your app → set expiry to Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
  4. Copy it once into Render as WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN. Leave the token boxes in Event settings empty.
